Photo Info Editor

Edit photo metadata without resizing or recompressing your original image. You can select individual photos or an entire folder.

Privacy: processing happens in this browser; your photos are not uploaded. JPEG pixels are kept as-is. Original EXIF capture date/time fields are preserved. A browser cannot preserve the operating system's file-created/modified timestamps on newly downloaded copies. The megapixel field is a metadata label only; it does not change actual resolution.
