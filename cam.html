Photo Metadata Editor

Update camera and ownership details in your JPEGs while keeping the original photo pixels and capture date/time intact.

1. Select your photos

2. Edit metadata

iPhone tip: after processing, tap Share to iPhone Photos, then choose Save Images in the iOS share sheet. If that option is unavailable, download the ZIP, open it in Files, extract it, then share the JPEGs to Photos.

Privacy: processing happens on your device; photos are not uploaded. Only JPEG metadata is changed, not the image pixels. Original EXIF capture date/time values are preserved. Browser downloads cannot retain the file-system created/modified timestamps. Megapixels is a metadata label only and does not change actual resolution.
